Independent senior developer

I enjoy learning the system you already have

I am a UK-based independent senior developer. I have been writing software since 2007 and contracting remotely since 2014.

I spend time on your live system and watch how people use it. Then I pick the next change that is worth making. I cover the server, the screens, the database, the infrastructure and the tests.

How I work

I talk to the people who need the change. A precise question saves a wasted build. Then I make something small enough to ship, usually by changing the system you already run.

I pick tools that fit the problem. I leave existing data alone. I look at the cloud bill and cut what is sitting idle. I tell you where things stand, in plain English, and I am still here after the merge.

What I take on

The work is usually both sides of a web product: screens and server, sign-in, the database, and the tests in the pipeline. I also take on older systems that have become hard to change or more expensive than they need to be.

Technologies

In production I have used:

  • C# / .NET, from Framework through .NET 8
  • SQL Server
  • Vue, Nuxt, Angular and React
  • TypeScript
  • Python
  • Azure and Terraform
  • Authentication and identity integration
  • Cypress
  • xUnit, MSTest and Moq
  • Azure DevOps

For my own web and Android work I have used TypeScript, Cloudflare Workers, R2 and Capgo.

Coding with agents

I write a brief and split the work into tasks. I give a task to Cursor or another coding agent. I read what comes back and change anything I would not have written myself. I click through the real user flow before I treat it as done. The architecture and what we keep in the long run are my decisions.
